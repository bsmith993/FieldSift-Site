Your photos stay yours

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

FieldSift does not collect or send your personal information or photo data to its developer. The app works with folders you choose and keeps its app data on your device.

Information FieldSift uses

FieldSift does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. The app does not send information to FieldSift developer-operated servers.

To browse your photos, FieldSift uses Android's system folder picker. It can read files and folders you grant access to, including photo contents and image metadata needed to display previews and photo information. The selected folder and its access permission may be remembered on your device so the app can reopen it next time.

Information stored on your device

FieldSift stores app preferences and culling data in its private app storage, including:

  • Photo ratings and Pick/Reject flags, associated with a photo's file name, size, and modified time.
  • Whether those ratings and flags have been synchronized to an XMP sidecar, and limited bookkeeping used to maintain that data.
  • Your selected folder reference, Plus/test settings, and other app preferences.

FieldSift does not store a central photo library or upload your ratings. App-private data is generally removed from the device when you uninstall the app. Android's device-backup feature may back up some app data according to your Android, device, and account settings; this backup is managed by Android or your device provider, not FieldSift. You can also clear the app's data through Android settings to remove locally stored ratings and preferences.

Actions that write or share files

If you choose to create XMP sidecars, FieldSift writes rating and flag information next to the related photos in the folder you selected. If you choose to back up a folder, it reads the selected source and writes copies to the destination you selected. Optional backup verification compares file sizes and SHA-256 checksums on your device. FieldSift does not send those files or checksums to its developer.

If you choose to share photos, FieldSift may prepare JPEG files in temporary app storage and passes them to Android's share interface for the recipient app you select. Temporary exports are eligible for cleanup after 24 hours; Android may remove cached files sooner. JPEGs that FieldSift copies unchanged may retain metadata already embedded in the original, such as camera or location metadata. Once you share a file, the receiving app's handling of it is governed by that app's own terms and privacy policy.

Storage providers and other apps

Android storage providers (including cloud-storage providers) and apps opened through the share interface are separate services that FieldSift does not operate. A provider may upload, process, or retain files according to its own settings and privacy policy. For example, a backup written to a cloud provider may be uploaded by that provider; FieldSift cannot control or confirm the provider's upload or retention practices.

Notifications and background backup

When a backup is running, Android may show an ongoing notification with backup progress. FieldSift uses Android's foreground-service and wake-lock features to let a backup continue while the screen is off. Notification permission can be controlled in Android settings.

Your choices

  • Choose which folders FieldSift can access, or revoke access, in Android's system settings.
  • Use the app's controls to decide whether to share, back up, delete, or write sidecars for files.
  • Clear FieldSift's app data or uninstall it to remove locally stored app data.
  • Manage copies, sidecars, and shared files separately in the storage provider or receiving app where they were saved.

Children's privacy

FieldSift is a general-purpose photo utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if FieldSift's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the FieldSift project through the GitHub issue tracker.
