Made for the photo-card review

Browse · review · decide

A focused workflow for your next keepers.

Get from a full camera card to a considered selection without importing everything into a desktop workflow first.

▦

Browse the card

Choose a card folder with Android's system picker and scan supported photo folders into a date-grouped grid.

◉

Review RAW previews

Open photos full-screen, zoom in, and inspect camera and exposure information. RAW previews come from the image embedded in the file.

★

Mark your picks

Rate photos from one to five stars, flag Picks or Rejects, and filter the grid to focus on the images that matter.

⌫

Cull with care

Review rejected shots and confirm before deleting them from the selected card. Marks stay on your device.

▤

Work with camera formats

Browse common RAW formats alongside JPEG and HEIF photos, including RAW+JPEG pairs.

⌁

Your folders, your choice

FieldSift accesses folders you select. Your photos are not uploaded to FieldSift developer servers.

A simple field workflow

Pick a card. Review at your pace.

Connect a supported card reader or camera, choose the folder, then browse and mark photos on your Android device.

Photo formats

RAW: CR3, CR2, CRW, DNG, NEF, NRW, ARW, RAF, ORF, RW2, PEF, SRW. Also JPG/JPEG, HEIC/HEIF, and HIF.

  1. 1Connect and chooseUse Android's folder picker to grant access to the card folder you want to browse.
  2. 2Scan and inspectSee matching photos in a date-grouped grid. Open a photo to zoom and check its details.
  3. 3Rate and cullSet ratings and flags, filter your selection, and confirm before removing rejected files.
